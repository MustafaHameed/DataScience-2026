\large\itshape\color{secondary!80!black}
An agent that has a goal but cannot look ahead is stuck with the first action that
seems sensible. Part~II is how it looks ahead: systematically when it knows nothing,
selectively when it can estimate distance to the goal, and --- when the route does
not matter and only the destination does --- by improving one state until it cannot
be improved.
\end{tcolorbox}
