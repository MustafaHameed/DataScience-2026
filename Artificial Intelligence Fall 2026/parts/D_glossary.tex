\normalfigures

Every key term of the course, with the chapter that introduces it.

\rowcolors{2}{white}{lightbg}
\begin{longtable}{@{}L{3.6cm}L{10.2cm}L{1.0cm}@{}}
\toprule
\rowcolor{primary!15}
\textbf{Term} & \textbf{Meaning} & \textbf{Ch.} \\
\midrule
\endhead
Admissible & A heuristic that never overestimates the remaining cost. Makes
$A^{*}$ optimal & 5 \\
Agent & A program that perceives an environment and acts on it & 1 \\
Agent function & The mapping from every percept sequence to an action. Usually
too large to tabulate & 2 \\
Agent program & A bounded implementation of an agent function & 2 \\
Alpha--beta pruning & Discarding branches of a game tree that cannot affect the
minimax value & 8 \\
Arc consistency & Every value of a variable has a consistent partner in a
neighbouring variable. Enforced by AC-3 & 7 \\
$A^{*}$ & Best-first search ordered by $f(n) = g(n) + h(n)$ & 5 \\
Backtracking & Depth-first search that assigns one variable at a time and undoes
the assignment on failure & 7 \\
Backward chaining & Inference from a goal towards the facts that would establish
it & 13 \\
Bayesian network & A directed acyclic graph whose edges encode conditional
independence, with a probability table per node & 15 \\
Bayes' rule & $P(a \mid b) = P(b \mid a)P(a)/P(b)$ & 14 \\
Branching factor & The average number of successors of a state, written $b$ & 4 \\
Brittleness & Failing catastrophically rather than gracefully just outside the
region of competence & 21 \\
Clause & A disjunction of literals; the unit resolution operates on & 11 \\
Combinatorial explosion & Exponential growth of a search space with depth. What
ended the first wave of AI & 1, 4 \\
Completeness (search) & The guarantee of finding a solution if one exists & 4 \\
Completeness (logic) & Everything entailed can be derived by the procedure & 11 \\
Conflict resolution & Choosing which of several applicable rules to fire & 13 \\
Conjunctive normal form & A conjunction of disjunctions of literals & 11 \\
Consistent & A heuristic with $h(n) \le c(n,n') + h(n')$. Needed for graph search
& 5 \\
Constraint satisfaction problem & Variables, domains and constraints; a solution
assigns every variable without violating any constraint & 7 \\
Defuzzification & Converting an aggregated fuzzy output to a single number,
usually by centroid & 16 \\
Degree of belief & Probability read as a measure of confidence rather than of
frequency & 14 \\
Depth-first search & Expanding the deepest unexpanded node. Cheap in memory,
neither complete nor optimal & 4 \\
Dominance & $h_{2} \ge h_{1}$ everywhere, so $A^{*}$ expands no more nodes with
$h_{2}$ & 5 \\
DPLL & Model checking with unit propagation and pure-literal elimination & 11 \\
Eliza effect & Attributing understanding to a system that is matching patterns &
19 \\
Entailment & $\mathit{KB} \models \alpha$: $\alpha$ is true in every model of the
knowledge base & 11 \\
Evaluation function & An estimate of a game position's value, used when search is
cut off & 8 \\
Expected utility & $\sum_{s} P(s \mid a) U(s)$; the quantity a rational agent
maximises & 14 \\
Explanation & The chain of facts and rules that produced a conclusion & 13, 21 \\
Explored set & States already generated, held so they are not expanded twice &
4 \\
First-order logic & Logic with objects, relations and quantifiers & 12 \\
Forward chaining & Inference from the known facts towards whatever follows & 13 \\
Forward checking & Removing values inconsistent with the assignment just made &
7 \\
Frame & A named bundle of slots with default values & 10 \\
Frontier & Nodes generated but not yet expanded & 3, 4 \\
Fuzzy set & A set with graded membership $\mu_{A}(x) \in [0,1]$ & 16 \\
Genetic algorithm & Local search over a population, with selection, crossover and
mutation & 6 \\
Goal test & A predicate identifying goal states; not necessarily a single state &
3 \\
Greedy best-first search & Ordering the frontier by $h$ alone. Fast, not optimal &
5 \\
Heuristic & An estimate $h(n)$ of the cost remaining from $n$ to a goal & 5 \\
Hill climbing & Moving to the best neighbour until none is better & 6 \\
Horizon effect & Pushing an inevitable loss just beyond the search cutoff & 8 \\
Inference engine & The component that applies rules to working memory & 13 \\
Iterative deepening & Repeated depth-limited search. Breadth-first guarantees at
depth-first memory cost & 4 \\
Knowledge acquisition bottleneck & Rules must be elicited from experts one at a
time. What ended the expert-system boom & 1, 13 \\
Linear separability & Whether a single straight boundary can separate two
classes. The perceptron's limit & 17 \\
Local maximum & A state with no better neighbour that is not the best state & 6 \\
Means--ends analysis & Choosing an operator that reduces a difference between the
current state and the goal & 9 \\
Minimax & The value of a game position under optimal play by both sides & 8 \\
Minimum remaining values & Assign next the variable with the fewest legal values.
Fails fast & 7 \\
Model & An assignment of truth values under which a sentence can be evaluated &
11 \\
Node & A state plus parent, action and cost. Not the same as a state & 3 \\
Occurs check & Refusing to bind a variable to a term containing it & 12 \\
Ontology & A shared, formal vocabulary of the types in a domain and their
relations & 10 \\
PEAS & Performance measure, environment, actuators, sensors & 2 \\
Percept & What the agent senses at one instant & 1, 2 \\
Perceptron & A single linear threshold unit, trained by the perceptron rule & 17 \\
Plateau & A flat region of the search landscape; a shoulder if it leads upward &
6 \\
Production rule & A condition--action pair in a rule base & 13 \\
Rationality & Maximising expected performance given the percepts so far. Not
omniscience, not perfection & 2 \\
Relaxation & Dropping a constraint to obtain an admissible heuristic cheaply &
5 \\
Resolution & From $(\alpha \vee \ell)$ and $(\beta \vee \neg\ell)$ infer
$(\alpha \vee \beta)$ & 11, 12 \\
Ridge & A run of local maxima along which no single move improves & 6 \\
Semantic network & A graph of concepts and labelled relations, queried by
inheritance & 10 \\
Simulated annealing & Accepting worsening moves with probability
$\exp(\Delta/T)$, with $T$ falling to zero & 6 \\
Skolemisation & Replacing an existentially quantified variable with a function of
the universals enclosing it & 12 \\
Soundness & Everything derived is entailed & 11 \\
State & A value describing a situation. Immutable and hashable & 3 \\
State space & The graph of states and the actions between them & 4 \\
STRIPS & An action representation: preconditions, add list, delete list & 9 \\
Unification & Finding a substitution that makes two expressions identical & 12 \\
Uniform-cost search & Expanding the cheapest path first. Optimal; Dijkstra's
algorithm & 4 \\
Utility & A number expressing how much an outcome is worth & 14 \\
Value of perfect information & The most an agent should pay to learn something
before deciding & 14 \\
Working memory & The facts currently believed by a production system & 13 \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
