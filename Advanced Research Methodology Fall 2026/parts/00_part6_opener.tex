Research that is not written is not research. This part is procedural
by design: the synopsis in the form your Departmental Research Committee
expects, the thesis and the paper, the submission and the reviewers, and the
defence. It is placed last because it depends on everything before it, and it
is taught in one workshop week because by this point you should be executing
decisions already made rather than making them.

\begin{conceptbox}[What Part~6 Covers]
\begin{tight}
  \item \textbf{Chapter 30 --- The Synopsis.} The IUB structure, populated, and the approval path from supervisor to AS\&RB.
  \item \textbf{Chapter 31 --- Writing the Thesis and the Paper.} IMRaD, the abstract, tables and figures, and the reporting quality criteria you are judged on.
  \item \textbf{Chapter 32 --- Publishing.} Venue selection, submission, responding to reviewers, and surviving rejection.
  \item \textbf{Chapter 33 --- Presenting and Defending.} The comprehensive examination, the synopsis defence, the viva, and the four verdicts.
\end{tight}
\end{conceptbox}
