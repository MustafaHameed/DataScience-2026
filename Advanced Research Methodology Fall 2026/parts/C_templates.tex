written on.

\section{C17 --- Pre-Submission Audit}

Work this the week before you submit the synopsis or the thesis. Every ``no''
is a question you will be asked.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{0.8cm}L{13.0cm}@{}}
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textbf{The argument}}\\[2pt]
$\square$ & The contribution is stated in one sentence, in the abstract and the
            conclusion, in the same words \\
$\square$ & Every research question has an answer in the results \\
$\square$ & Every result bears on a research question; anything that does not
            is cut or moved to an appendix \\
$\square$ & No claim exceeds what the design supports --- including in the
            title (\chref{quasiexp}) \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textbf{Method and analysis}}\\[2pt]
$\square$ & The design is justified against at least one rejected alternative \\
$\square$ & Sample size is justified by power analysis or saturation evidence \\
$\square$ & Every measure has stated reliability or validity evidence \\
$\square$ & Effect sizes with intervals reported, not only p-values \\
$\square$ & Multiplicity handled and stated \\
$\square$ & Deviations from the plan tabulated with reasons (\chref{prereg}) \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textbf{Threats}}\\[2pt]
$\square$ & All four validity types addressed, in order \\
$\square$ & Each threat graded mitigated / measured / acknowledged \\
$\square$ & Each acknowledged threat has a direction \\
$\square$ & The most serious threat is named first, not buried \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textbf{Integrity and compliance}}\\[2pt]
$\square$ & Similarity report run with references removed; every match
            explicable \\
$\square$ & Overall $\le$19\%, no single source $\ge$5\%, findings and
            conclusions $\le$9\% \\
$\square$ & Every citation corresponds to a paper you have opened \\
$\square$ & AI-use disclosure completed (C15) \\
$\square$ & Ethics approval reference included; consent forms in an appendix \\
$\square$ & Authorship agreed in writing; first-author requirement met for any
            \S15 paper \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textbf{Artefacts and format}}\\[2pt]
$\square$ & Data, code and environment deposited; DOI cited (C13) \\
$\square$ & Raw data prepared for the supervisor as \S14(i) requires \\
$\square$ & Formatting matches the IUB guidelines --- \S14.2(vi), and the
            commonest cause of a return \\
$\square$ & Table of contents, lists and cross-references regenerated \\
$\square$ & Spell-checked, and read aloud once end to end \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{alertbox}[The last item is not padding]
Reading the whole document aloud is slow and it is the single most effective
proofreading technique there is. You will hear the clumsy sentence structure
\reg{PhD Regs 2024}{\S14.2(iv)} explicitly asks to have been addressed, and you
will catch the paragraph that says the same thing twice.

\medskip
Budget a full day. It is a better use of the last week than another pass over
the related work.
\end{alertbox}
