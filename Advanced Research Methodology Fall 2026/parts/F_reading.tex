\chapter{Annotated Reading List}\label{app:reading}
%=====================================================================

The anchor readings, by module, with what to take from each. Full details are
in the References at the end of the volume; the \texttt{refs.bib} shipped
alongside this handout contains all of them, ready to import into Zotero or
JabRef (\chref{bibliometrics}).

\begin{alertbox}[On the access labels]
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{3.4cm}L{10.7cm}@{}}
\toprule
\oa{} \textbf{Open access} & The publisher's own version is free to read \\
\textbf{Author copy}       & Free from an author or institutional page, but the
                             publisher version is paywalled. \emph{This is not
                             open access}, and this book does not call it that \\
\textbf{Preprint}          & A preprint is freely available; the published
                             version may differ \\
\textbf{Library}           & Paywalled. Use your institutional subscription \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\medskip
The distinction is laboured for a reason. A book that teaches you to state
provenance precisely should state its own precisely
(\chref{integrity}).

\medskip
One caution about the \oa{} label itself. Some of these are free on the
publisher's site without carrying an open licence --- what Unpaywall calls
\emph{bronze} access. You may read them; you may not assume you can
redistribute them, and the publisher can withdraw them without notice.
Genuinely licensed open access says so on the article page, usually as a
Creative Commons mark. Check before you reuse a figure
(\chref{publishing}).
\end{alertbox}

\begin{conceptbox}[Verify before you rely]
Access status changes: journals flip to open access, author copies disappear,
preprints are superseded. Every entry here carries a DOI in
\texttt{refs.bib}; resolve it rather than trusting the label, and correct the
label in your own copy when you find it has changed.

\medskip
That is not a disclaimer. It is the practice this book asks of you everywhere
else, applied to itself.
\end{conceptbox}
