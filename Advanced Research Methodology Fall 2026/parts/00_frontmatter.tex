\chapter*{How to Use This Book}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{How to Use This Book}
\markboth{How to Use This Book}{}

A research methods course fails in a characteristic way. Students can recite
the difference between a case study and an experiment, and still cannot say
what would have to be true for their own study to be wrong. This book is
organised to prevent that. Every chapter ends by asking what would falsify
the claim it just taught you to make.

\section*{The Argument in Six Parts}

\begin{tight}
  \item \textbf{Part I} establishes what a contribution to computing knowledge
    actually is, and how a vague interest becomes a question that evidence can
    answer.
  \item \textbf{Part II} is about the literature: finding it systematically,
    appraising it honestly, and synthesising it into something that did not
    exist before you read it.
  \item \textbf{Part III} is the catalogue of designs --- design science,
    experiments, repository mining, case studies, surveys, qualitative work,
    mixed methods, benchmarking --- and the grounds for choosing between them.
  \item \textbf{Part IV} is measurement and analysis: making a construct
    observable, and reasoning correctly from the numbers that result.
  \item \textbf{Part V} is what separates research from advocacy ---
    reproducibility, pre-registration, ethics, integrity, and the rules
    governing generative AI in your own work.
  \item \textbf{Part VI} is production: the synopsis, the thesis, the paper,
    and the defence.
\end{tight}

\begin{alertbox}[Two Conventions That Matter]
\textbf{Worked studies are illustrative.} Every study in a
\emph{Worked Study} box is constructed for teaching. The numbers are invented
and are labelled as such. No result in this book is attributed to a real
published study unless it carries a citation.

\medskip
\textbf{Open access is claimed only where it is true.} A reading marked
\oa{} has a publisher version that is free to read. An author copy posted on a
personal page is not open access, and this book does not pretend otherwise ---
which is itself part of the lesson.
\end{alertbox}
