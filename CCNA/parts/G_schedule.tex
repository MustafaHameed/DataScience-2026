\section{A sixteen-week schedule}

Two sessions a week --- one of theory, one at a keyboard --- with the lab session
treated as the more important of the two. The blueprint is 55\% configure,
troubleshoot and diagnose, and a course that teaches this material without
hardware or a simulator is teaching the smaller fraction of the exam.

\begin{conceptbox}[Where chapters 33 and 34 go]
Not in a week of their own. \chref{tshootmethod} should be introduced in week~8
alongside Chapter~14 and then \emph{used} in every lab after it --- the method is
worthless as a lecture and valuable as a habit. \chref{examstrategy} belongs in
the last session before the mock, when the constraints it describes are about to
become real.
\end{conceptbox}

\begin{alertbox}[If you have twelve weeks, not sixteen]
Do not cut the labs. Cut in this order, and be honest with students about what
they are getting:

\begin{tight}
  \item Merge weeks 15 and 16 into one, and set \chref{wireless},
        \chref{virtualisation} and \chref{ai} as directed reading with a quiz.
        They are \emph{describe} topics, which is the only part of this blueprint
        that survives being read rather than done.
  \item Merge \chref{ospfv3} into \chref{ospfv2}'s session --- it is written as a
        delta and takes forty minutes.
  \item Merge \chref{vpn} into \chref{acl}'s week. It has no lab by design.
\end{tight}

What must not be cut: \chref{tshootl2}, \chref{tshootmethod}, and the fault
injection in every lab. That is the 28\% of the blueprint that begins
\emph{troubleshoot} or \emph{diagnose}, and it cannot be delivered any other way.
\end{alertbox}

\section{Lab index}

Thirty-two labs. Every one states its platform in its header; Parts~I to~III run
in Packet Tracer, and from Part~IV the labs move to CML or GNS3 where real
platform behaviour matters. Chapter~1 has no lab because it has nothing to
configure yet, and \chref{vpn} has none because topic~\tp{4.5} is
\emph{describe}.

\begin{conceptbox}[Regenerating this table]
The lab index and Appendix~\ref{app:coverage} are both produced by
\cmd{tools/gen\_appendices.py}, which reads the chapter sources. Re-run it after
adding, moving or retagging a chapter, and paste the table back here. Nothing in
either table is typed by hand, so neither can drift from the chapters they
describe.
