\chapter{Glossary}\label{app:glossary}

Terms marked as key terms in the chapters, defined in one or two lines. Where a
term is easy to confuse with another, the contrast is given rather than just the
definition --- those pairs are where the exam questions live.

\begin{longtable}{@{}L{4.0cm}L{11.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
\rowcolor{primary!15}
\textbf{Term} & \textbf{Meaning} \\
\midrule
\endhead

\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textcolor{primary}{\textbf{A--B}}} \\
\midrule
AAA & Authentication (who are you), authorization (what may you do), accounting
(what did you do). Three separate questions answered by ordered method lists \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
A record & DNS: name to IPv4 address. \textbf{AAAA} is name to IPv6 address \\
Access port & A switch port carrying one untagged VLAN, for an end device \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Administrative distance & How much a router trusts a \emph{source} of routing
information. Connected 0, static 1, OSPF 110. Decides between sources; longest
prefix match decides between entries \\
Agentic AI & A system that pursues a goal, uses tools to act, and evaluates its
own results. Contrast \textbf{generative AI}, which answers and stops \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Agentless & Needs no software installed on the managed device. Why Ansible works
on switches and Puppet does not \\
AH & IPsec Authentication Header, IP protocol 51. Integrity and authentication,
\textbf{no encryption}, and incompatible with NAT \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
APIPA & A host's self-assigned \cmd{169.254.x.x} address. Means it asked for a
lease and nothing answered \\
ARP & Resolves an IP address to a MAC address on the local segment. Has no
authentication, which is why \textbf{DAI} exists \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Attenuation & Loss of signal strength with distance or through material \\
BPDU & Bridge Protocol Data Unit --- the frame spanning tree uses to elect a root
and detect loops \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
BPDU guard & Err-disables a port if a BPDU arrives on it. Applied to access ports,
where none should ever appear \\
Blast radius & How much breaks if an action is wrong. Governs how much autonomy
an automated system should be given \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Block size & 256 minus the interesting octet of a subnet mask. Subnets begin at
multiples of it \\
Broadcast domain & The set of devices a broadcast reaches. One per VLAN \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
BSSID & The MAC address of one radio on one access point --- what a client is
actually associated to. Contrast \textbf{SSID}, the network's name \\

\midrule
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textcolor{primary}{\textbf{C--D}}} \\
\midrule
CAM table & A switch's MAC address table: which MAC was last seen on which port \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
CDP & Cisco Discovery Protocol. Reveals directly connected Cisco devices.
\textbf{LLDP} is the vendor-neutral equivalent, and is off by default \\
Channel bonding & Combining adjacent channels for more throughput. Halves the
number of non-overlapping channels and raises the noise floor \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
CNAME & DNS: name to another \emph{name}. A name that is a CNAME cannot hold any
other record, which is why an MX must not point at one \\
Co-channel interference & Two APs on the \emph{same} channel. They hear each
other and share airtime. \textbf{Better} than adjacent-channel interference,
where they cannot hear each other and corrupt each other's frames \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Collision domain & The set of devices that can collide with one another. One per
switch port on a modern network \\
Community string & SNMP v1 and v2c's credential, sent in \textbf{clear text} in
every packet. Renaming it from \cmd{public} protects nothing \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Container & An isolated user-space environment sharing the \textbf{host's
kernel}. Megabytes, milliseconds to start, weaker isolation than a VM \\
Controller-based & A controller holds intended state and programs devices.
Contrast \textbf{automation-based}, which adds tooling but no control plane \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
DAI & Dynamic ARP Inspection. Validates ARP against the DHCP snooping binding
table, so statically addressed hosts need an ARP ACL \\
Data classification & Deciding what data may be included in a prompt. Listed
first among the four prompt components, and the one that can disqualify a
prompt outright \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
DHCP snooping & Drops DHCP \emph{server} messages on untrusted ports, and builds
the binding table that DAI depends on \\
DORA & Discover, Offer, Request, Ack. The first three are broadcast, which is why
relays exist \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
DR / BDR & OSPF's designated and backup designated router on a multi-access
segment. Elected by priority, then highest router ID. Not pre-emptive \\
DTP & Dynamic Trunking Protocol. Negotiates trunks automatically; disable with
\cmd{switchport nonegotiate} \\

\midrule
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textcolor{primary}{\textbf{E--H}}} \\
\midrule
EAP & The authentication framework behind WPA-Enterprise and 802.1X \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Err-disabled & A port shut down by a protection feature. Recover with
\cmd{shutdown} then \cmd{no shutdown}, or \cmd{errdisable recovery} \\
ESP & IPsec Encapsulating Security Payload, IP protocol 50. The one that
\textbf{encrypts}, and the one everyone uses \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Established & An ACL keyword matching TCP segments with ACK or RST set --- replies
to conversations already started. Inspects flags, so it is \emph{not} stateful \\
EtherChannel & Several physical links treated as one logical link. Negotiated by
LACP, or forced with \cmd{mode on} \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
EUI-64 & Building an IPv6 interface ID from a MAC: split it, insert \cmd{fffe},
flip the seventh bit \\
Extended ping & A ping with a chosen source, size, count or DF bit. Finds faults
a plain ping cannot \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Facility & The subsystem in a syslog message ---
\cmd{\%LINEPROTO} in \cmd{\%LINEPROTO-5-UPDOWN} \\
Floating static & A static route with a raised administrative distance, so it
installs only when the preferred route disappears \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Giaddr & The gateway IP address field a DHCP relay fills in. Selects the pool and
carries the reply back \\
Global unicast & A routable IPv6 address, currently from \cmd{2000::/3}.
Contrast \textbf{link-local} (\cmd{fe80::/10}), which exists on every interface \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
HSRP & Cisco's first hop redundancy protocol. Virtual IP and virtual MAC shared
between routers; \textbf{not} pre-emptive unless configured \\

\midrule
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textcolor{primary}{\textbf{I--M}}} \\
\midrule
Idempotent & Produces the same result applied once or many times. Why an Ansible
playbook is safe to re-run \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
IKE & Internet Key Exchange, UDP 500 (4500 with NAT-T). Phase 1 builds a secure
channel between peers; phase 2 protects user traffic \\
Implicit deny & The invisible \cmd{deny any} at the end of every ACL. Permit what
you need and everything else stops \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Inform & An SNMP notification that is \textbf{acknowledged} and retransmitted.
Contrast a \textbf{trap}, which is sent once over UDP and may be lost \\
Inside local / global & The internal host's private address / the public address
it is translated to. \textbf{Outside local} and \textbf{outside global} are
usually the same address \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Link-local next hop & An OSPFv3 or IPv6 static route's \cmd{fe80::} next hop. It
is correct, and it requires the interface to be named alongside it \\
LLDP & Vendor-neutral neighbour discovery. \textbf{Off by default} \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Longest prefix match & The most specific matching route wins, regardless of
administrative distance or metric. The first rule of forwarding \\
Loop guard & Blocks a port that stops receiving BPDUs, preventing a
unidirectional link from opening a loop \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
MAC randomisation & Operating systems presenting a random per-network MAC.
\textbf{On by default} everywhere, and it breaks MAC-based authorisation and
DHCP reservations \\
Method list & An ordered list of AAA methods. A method is skipped only when it
\emph{does not respond}; a rejection ends the list \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
MIB / OID & The tree of variables an SNMP agent exposes, and the numeric
identifier of one of them \\
Mnemonic & The event name in a syslog message --- \cmd{UPDOWN} in
\cmd{\%LINEPROTO-5-UPDOWN} \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
MX & DNS: domain to mail exchanger, with a preference number. Must name a host
with an A record, and must not name a CNAME \\

\midrule
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textcolor{primary}{\textbf{N--R}}} \\
\midrule
Native VLAN & The untagged VLAN on an 802.1Q trunk. Must match at both ends \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
NAT-T & NAT traversal. Wraps ESP in UDP 4500 so PAT can handle it \\
Noise floor & Background RF energy on a channel. Signal strength without it tells
you nothing --- \textbf{SNR} is what decides whether a link works \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
NS record & DNS: the servers authoritative for a zone. A fault here takes out
everything in the domain at once \\
NXDOMAIN & The name does not exist. Contrast \cmd{NOERROR} with an empty answer
section, which means the name exists but not with that record type \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Orchestration & Scheduling, health-checking and scaling containers across hosts.
Kubernetes is the dominant one \\
Overload & The keyword that turns dynamic NAT into PAT by translating ports as
well as addresses. Its absence is why NAT ``stops working after lunch'' \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
PAT & Port Address Translation. Many internal hosts behind one public address \\
Persona & The prompt component setting the expertise and platform to answer from \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
PoE & Power over Ethernet. Budget matters: a switch's total power is finite \\
PortFast & Moves an access port straight to forwarding, skipping listening and
learning. Use with BPDU guard \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Port group & A named VLAN and policy on a virtual switch, that VMs attach to \\
Port security & Limits which and how many MAC addresses a port accepts.
Violation modes \cmd{protect} (silent --- avoid), \cmd{restrict} (logs),
\cmd{shutdown} (default) \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
PTR & DNS: address to name, in \cmd{in-addr.arpa}. Usually controlled by whoever
owns the address block, and its absence gets outbound mail rejected \\
RA guard & Drops IPv6 router advertisements on ports classified as hosts. The
IPv6 counterpart of DHCP snooping \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
RADIUS & UDP 1812/1813. Encrypts only the password; combines authentication and
authorization. For \textbf{network access}. Contrast \textbf{TACACS+} \\
Rapid PVST+ & Cisco's per-VLAN rapid spanning tree \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Root guard & Refuses a superior BPDU on a port, so a rogue switch cannot become
root \\
RSSI & Received signal strength in dBm, always negative. $-67$ or better for
voice, $-70$ for data \\

\midrule
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textcolor{primary}{\textbf{S--Z}}} \\
\midrule
SAE & WPA3's key exchange, replacing WPA2's 4-way handshake. Resists offline
dictionary attack and gives forward secrecy \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Save\_when & The Ansible \cmd{ios\_config} option that writes to startup-config.
Without it, a change survives only until the next reload \\
SCP / SFTP & File transfer inside SSH. IOS can be an SCP \emph{server} and an
SFTP \emph{client}. Both replace TFTP, which authenticates nobody \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Severity & The digit in a syslog message, 0 (emergency) to 7 (debugging).
\textbf{0 is worst.} A threshold of \emph{n} records 0 through \emph{n} \\
SLAAC & IPv6 hosts building their own address from a router advertisement \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
SNR & The gap in dB between signal and noise. \textbf{The number that decides
whether a wireless link works.} 25 dB for voice, 20 dB minimum for data \\
Sticky MAC & Port security converting learned addresses into configuration ---
which is \textbf{not saved} until you \cmd{write memory} \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Storm control & Caps broadcast, multicast or unknown unicast as a share of a
port's bandwidth. Rising and falling thresholds prevent oscillation \\
SVI & Switch Virtual Interface. A VLAN's layer 3 interface on a switch \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
TACACS+ & TCP 49. Encrypts the \textbf{entire} packet, separates AAA fully,
supports per-command authorization. For \textbf{device administration} \\
Trunk & A link carrying several VLANs, tagged with 802.1Q. \cmd{allowed vlan}
\textbf{replaces} the list; \cmd{allowed vlan add} extends it \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Trusted port & A port on which DHCP server messages, or ARP, are accepted without
validation. Uplinks must be trusted or a whole switch fails \\
TTL & Two meanings. In DNS, how long a record may be cached --- which is why a
correct fix takes time to appear. In IP, a hop counter \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Tunnel mode & IPsec encrypting the \textbf{entire original packet} and adding a
new IP header. The normal case. Contrast \textbf{transport mode}, which keeps the
original header \\
Type 1 / type 2 & A hypervisor running on the bare metal / running on a host
operating system \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Virtual switch & The switch inside a hypervisor. Does not run spanning tree, and
its uplink to the physical network is a \textbf{trunk} \\
VRRP & The standards-based first hop redundancy protocol. Pre-empts by default,
unlike HSRP \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
Wildcard mask & The inverse of a subnet mask: 0 means must match, 1 means ignore.
Network address plus wildcard equals the broadcast address \\
WPA2 / WPA3 & AES-CCMP with a 4-way handshake / AES-GCMP with SAE.
\textbf{Personal} shares a passphrase; \textbf{Enterprise} authenticates each
user against RADIUS \\
\rowcolor{lightbg}
YAML & The format Ansible playbooks are written in. Spaces not tabs; \cmd{-} for
list items; a space after every colon \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
