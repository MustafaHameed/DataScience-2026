\vspace*{0.4cm}
Before you can configure a network you have to know what is moving through it,
how it is addressed, and how to talk to a device at all. Part~I builds that
foundation from nothing.

It is also where two of the exam's heaviest habits are formed. The first is
\term{subnetting on paper}, quickly and without a calculator --- topic \tp{1.3}
asks you to troubleshoot addressing, and you cannot troubleshoot what you cannot
compute. The second is \term{reading device output}, which is what topic \tp{2.4}
tests and what every troubleshooting question in the paper depends on.
