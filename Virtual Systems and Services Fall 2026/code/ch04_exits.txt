=====================================================================
STEP 6  --  two thousand forks, five seconds
=====================================================================
 Performance counter stats for process id '48217':

            17,219      kvm:kvm_exit

       5.002441883 seconds time elapsed

  THE POINT. Two thousand processes created, each rewriting page tables,
  and the exit rate is BELOW the idle rate per second. Under shadow
  paging this would have been tens of thousands of exits per second,
  because every guest page-table write would have trapped. Extended page
  tables let the guest edit its own tables with ordinary memory writes.

  That is the worked example's claim, measured.
