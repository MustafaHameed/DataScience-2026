STEP 5  --  the measurement that matters
=====================================================================
alone on one instance:
  41.8 s

all three instances busy together:
  42.1 s
  41.9 s
  42.3 s

  THE POINT. The single-instance time and the all-busy times agree to
  within 1%. The partitions are enforced in hardware, so a neighbour
  cannot take your streaming multiprocessors.

For comparison, the same test under time-sliced vGPU (4 users):
alone:                    41.6 s
all four busy together:  166.4 s   159.8 s   171.2 s   163.5 s

  Four times slower, and varying by 7% between users. Same throughput
  overall; no guarantee to any individual. That difference is what
  Chapter 20's worked example is choosing between.

STEP 6  --  what "utilisation" actually reports
=====================================================================
$ nvidia-smi --query-gpu=utilization.gpu,utilization.memory,\
memory.used,memory.total --format=csv -l 1 -c 5
utilization.gpu [%], utilization.memory [%], memory.used [MiB], memory.total [MiB]
100 %, 12 %, 18244 MiB, 81920 MiB
100 %, 11 %, 18244 MiB, 81920 MiB
100 %, 13 %, 18244 MiB, 81920 MiB
100 %, 12 %, 18244 MiB, 81920 MiB
100 %,  9 %, 18244 MiB, 81920 MiB

  Read these two columns together. utilization.gpu is 100% -- a kernel
  was resident in every sample. utilization.memory is 12% -- the memory
  interface is almost idle. The job is occupying the card without using
  much of it, which is exactly the case in checkpoint question 3.

  Nsight Compute on the same kernel reported:
