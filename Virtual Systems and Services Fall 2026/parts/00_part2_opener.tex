Part~I ended with a problem: the architecture could not be virtualized, and the
software workarounds were slow and fragile. Part~II is the answer, in the order
the industry found it --- first in silicon, then in four platforms built on that
silicon, then in the two resources every guest competes for, and finally in the
devices the guest thinks it owns.
